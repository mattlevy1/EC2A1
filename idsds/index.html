IDSDS stepper

A walk-through of iterated deletion of strictly dominated strategies (IDSDS) for two-player normal-form games. Player 1 chooses the row, Player 2 the column; each cell is a payoff pair (u1, u2). Each round deletes every strictly dominated strategy for both players at once (a mixture may be the dominator). The survivors are the rationalizable strategies.

  1. Pick a preset (start with Lecture 1) or generate a random game. To reproduce one, enter its seed (same size and filter) and click Load.
  2. Click Step to run one IDSDS round. Use Back, Reset, or Jump to end to move along the path.
  3. Highlighted rows/columns were deleted this round; muted ones went earlier. Expand a deletion to see the payoff comparisons.
