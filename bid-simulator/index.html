Bid simulator

A sealed-bid practice lab for Lecture 5. You draw one private value \(v\) and choose one bid \(b\). A bot rival uses a linear rule \(b_2 = a v_2\). The chart and simulation hold your \(v\) and \(b\) fixed and redraw the rival’s valuation. Values here are \(U[0,1000]\) (the lecture’s running example used 900).

  1. Pick first-price or second-price, and an opponent type (start with BNE).
  2. Your value stays fixed until you draw a new one. Try the shortcuts \(v/2\) (BNE shade) and \(v\) (truthful).
  3. The curve is interim expected surplus as a function of your bid. The histogram is 10,000 rival draws at the bid you have chosen.
